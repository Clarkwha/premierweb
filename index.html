Site-Level Positions

Sanitation Technician

Entry-level position with training provided. Responsible for executing cleaning protocols.

Apply Now
Requirements:
  • • Must be able to work in cold, wet, and physically demanding environments
  • • Ability to follow strict safety and food sanitation guidelines
  • • Must be able to lift up to 50 lbs and stand for extended periods
  • • Reliable transportation and punctual attendance required
  • • Attention to detail and commitment to quality
  • • Willingness to wear PPE (personal protective equipment) at all times
  • • Must be able to work in a team environment and follow instructions

Sanitation Supervisor

Supports the Site Manager by directly overseeing sanitation technicians, ensuring all cleaning tasks are completed safely, efficiently, and in compliance with food safety standards and facility protocols.

Apply Now
Requirements:
  • • 1+ year of experience in a sanitation or industrial cleaning role
  • • Good communication and organizational skills
  • • Knowledge of GMPs and food safety regulations
  • • Ability to work in cold, wet, and physically demanding environments
  • • Ability to train and motivate team members
  • • Ability to work night shifts, weekends, or holidays as needed

Site Manager

Oversees all sanitation operations at the facility, ensuring compliance with safety standards, managing staff, and maintaining audit readiness.

Apply Now
Requirements:
  • • Minimum 2 years of experience in a leadership or supervisory role (sanitation or production preferred)
  • • Strong understanding of food safety protocols and audit readiness standards
  • • Ability to work flexible hours, including nights or weekends as needed
  • • Excellent problem-solving skills
  • • Bilingual (English/Spanish) is a plus
